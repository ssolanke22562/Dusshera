The Legend of Sona (Apta Leaves)

According to Maharashtrian & Indian tradition, Kautsa's devotion prompted Lord Kuber to shower golden coins on Shami and Apta trees. Ever since, exchanging Apta leaves on Dussehra signifies gifting purest Gold and spreading boundless prosperity.

⚔️

Ayudha Puja & New Beginnings

Vijayadashami is considered the most auspicious day of the year for starting new businesses, purchasing vehicles, commencing learning (Vidyarambham), and invoking blessings on tools of craft and livelihood.
